REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL

RFP reference: RFP-2026-014
Issued by: Brackenfield Health Network
Response due: 2026-12-18

1. Background

Brackenfield Health Network runs hospitals and clinics with about 2,100 staff. We are looking for a security and IT management solution to protect and run our systems.

2. Requirements

Vendors must answer every numbered requirement and say plainly where a requirement is not met.

R-01 The solution shall detect and respond to threats across endpoints, servers and cloud workloads, and help analysts triage alerts.

R-02 Every automated alert or block made by an AI model must come with a plain-language explanation that an analyst can review.

R-03 All patient-related and security data must be stored and processed inside the country. Describe where data and AI processing take place.

R-04 The vendor shall describe options to run the solution, including its AI models, in the customer's own data centre.

R-05 The solution must create and update tickets in the customer's existing service desk tool, ServiceNow.

R-06 Describe how other service desk and collaboration tools can be connected through an open interface.

R-07 The solution shall support single sign-on and role-based access control.

R-08 Provide compliance report templates, including templates suited to healthcare regulation.

R-09 Describe automated patching and remediation, and how human approval is kept for high-risk changes.

R-10 The solution should discover and monitor connected medical devices.

R-11 Provide a mobile app for on-call staff to review and approve actions.

R-12 Describe backup and recovery capabilities, including tested recovery times.

R-13 The vendor shall provide support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with stated response targets for critical incidents.

R-14 Provide an implementation plan and timeline to reach full operation.

R-15 Describe the training provided for administrators and analysts.

R-16 Describe the pricing model and any charges based on data volume.

R-17 Provide three customer references, at least two of them in healthcare.

R-18 Describe how detection models are updated and how false alarms are reduced over time.

3. Submission

Responses must be received by the due date. Late responses will not be considered.
